Contact us

Is Yardbird free for general contractors?

Yes. Searching, viewing profiles and contacting companies is free with no sign-up. A free account lets you save favorites and build lists.

Does Yardbird verify licenses and insurance?

No. License, insurance and workers' compensation status are self-reported by each company. A paid plan does not mean Yardbird has verified them.

What happens if a company cancels its plan?

The listing is never deleted. It moves back to the Free plan automatically.

Can a company serve more than one city or state?

Yes. Choose every state, city or region you work in and your company appears in searches for each one, not only your office address.
